The Minimalist Tower has become the new icon of the urban skyline. Its design, focused on the purity of lines and the elimination of unnecessary ornaments, offers a visual respite in the bustling metropolis.

Photographing this building is a creative challenge: the interaction of light and shadow on its white and gray surfaces changes radically throughout the day.

Don't miss the view from the central plaza, where the exaggerated perspective accentuates its dynamic and contemporary character.
