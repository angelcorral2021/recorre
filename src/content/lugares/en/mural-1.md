The "Urban Abstraction" mural was created in 2025 by a local artists' collective with the goal of bringing life back to one of the grayest corners of the central district.

The artwork is distinguished by its vibrant use of color and geometric figures that intertwine to form silhouettes of human faces, representing the diversity and constant movement of the city.

If you visit at sunset, the sunlight hits the warm tones directly, creating a stunning photographic effect, ideal for capturing the urban essence.
